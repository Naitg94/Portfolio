Goyal Traders

A full business website built for a local grocery store — from first impression to store information, all in one responsive site.

Live Demo
naitg94.github.io/Goyal-Traders-2

Overview

Goyal Traders is a business website built for a neighborhood grocery store in Gaurella, Chhattisgarh. It introduces the store, builds trust with new visitors, and makes it effortless to call or find the shop — with a distinct animated entry screen that sets the tone before visitors reach the main site.

My Role

I designed and built the entire site solo — from the visual direction and layout to every line of markup, styling, and interaction. This was an end-to-end freelance build, covering everything from concept to a fully responsive, deployed website.

Technologies Used

HTML CSS JavaScript

Key Features

  • Animated splash / landing screen with an "Enter Store" transition into the main site
  • Responsive hero section with dual call-to-actions — Call Now and Get Directions
  • Business info card with owner details, phone number, and full store address
  • Trust indicators bar highlighting years of service and product range
  • "Why Choose Us" section with icon-led feature cards
  • Fully responsive layout, tested across desktop, tablet, and mobile

What I Contributed

Every part of this site — layout structure, typography, color system, responsive behavior, and the interactive landing sequence — was designed and coded by me. The goal was to give a small local business a genuinely modern web presence, with a first impression (the animated splash screen) that most local business sites don't have, backed by clear, practical information for real customers looking to call or visit the store.
